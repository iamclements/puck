Puck is a fast, private remote for your Apple TV. It talks straight to the TV over your Wi-Fi using Apple's own Companion Link protocol, so there is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking.

CONTROL
• A glass trackpad: tap an edge to step, swipe anywhere to pan and scroll, press the middle to select. Prefer buttons? Switch to a classic D-pad in Settings.
• Back, Home and Play/Pause, with the play button reflecting what is really playing.
• Volume up and down, plus a mute toggle.
• Power: tap for Control Centre, hold to wake a sleeping Apple TV.

MORE THAN ONE APPLE TV
• Finds every Apple TV on your network and keeps as many paired as you like. Switch rooms from one list.

EVERYTHING ELSE
• App drawer with icons, to launch any app on the TV.
• Type from your phone's keyboard, including a lock-screen reply field.
• Now Playing with artwork, a draggable scrubber, skip and a media notification with lock-screen controls.
• Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tiles and launcher shortcuts that jump straight to a specific Apple TV.
• Material You colours, light and dark themes, and layouts for tablets and foldables.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Puck stores pairing credentials encrypted on your phone and sends nothing to any server. The one network request that leaves your Wi-Fi is the app drawer looking up app icons from Apple's public iTunes Search API.
